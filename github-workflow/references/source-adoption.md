## Local consolidations

The two review modes and reviewer template come from the installed Superpowers review skills; their upstream source IDs remain historical provenance in the updater manifests. Specialist `gstack-review` and `ponytail-review` retain their identities. The two installed push skills are consolidated into `github-remote-push`; the two installed DevOps setup/operation skills are redistributed into `github-build` and `github-release` by responsibility.

Future publication routes all `github-*` skills to `harness-subdev/harness-github` and common `code-review` to `harness-subdev/harness-global`. Prior copies of the push/DevOps skills may still live in harness-front, harness-devops or harness-global; a later explicitly authorized publication must review coordinated additions/removals there. This local refactor does not migrate, delete, push or release anything remotely. Do not interpret historical source names as current local aliases.

A concurrent frontend refactor changed the personal prefix to `web-*` while this work ran. Its current naming policy is preserved alongside legacy front-name mapping for migration; repository names stay unchanged. Its renamed push entry, `web-reference-publish`, contained the same former front publication operation and is consolidated into `github-remote-push --front`, with the latest original preserved outside discovery. Other frontend files and observed dependency manifests remain outside this refactor's ownership.
